WHO WE ARE

Made for the curious.

Wanderly is a demonstration travel discovery website created to help visitors find inspiring beaches, temples, and countries. Our simple search experience makes destination ideas easy to explore.

Our mission is to make travel inspiration welcoming, approachable, and useful for everyone, whether you're planning your first journey or dreaming of the next one.

HOW WE WORK

Guidance for team members

01

Research carefully

Verify the destination name, location, and practical details before publishing content.

02

Respect images and sources

Use appropriately licensed imagery, credit sources when required, and write original descriptions.

03

Test every update

Check Home, About Us, Contact Us, and all three search categories before changes go live.

04

Support travelers clearly

Keep messages friendly, provide accurate information, and never promise unverified services.
